The thesis

From energy security to technology sovereignty.

PowerIn Urja's strategy is to create domestic manufacturing capability at the most structurally constrained points of the energy-transition and technology value chain. The platform is designed to move from solar critical materials into semiconductor, storage, hydrogen, photonics and mechatronics opportunities over time.

Why it matters

01

Critical upstream capacity

Focuses on ingots and wafers — the upstream layer on which downstream solar cell and module manufacturing depends.

02

Designed for N-Type

Czochralski mono-crystal growth and diamond-wire wafering are aligned to TOPCon, HJT and next-generation tandem-compatible products.

03

Built for optionality

Campus infrastructure is intended to support future semiconductor, energy storage, hydrogen, photonics and mechatronics programmes.

About the campus

A platform, not a single plant.

PowerIn Urja India Private Limited is developing the PowerIn Integrated Manufacturing Campus at MIDC Additional Butibori, Nagpur. The wider campus is planned across 300+ acres, beginning with advanced solar ingot and wafer manufacturing.

Company formed; PIMC concept developed; state / location, technology and OEM partners selected; government approvals and incentives pursued.

Further expansion of ingot / wafering, solar cell capability and additional product lines based on business case and technology readiness.
