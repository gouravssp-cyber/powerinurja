04 · Founder's Vision & Mission

Technology sovereignty is built through long-term national capability.

PowerIn Urja's industrial platform sits within a wider vision for economic strength, human development, environmental quality and strategic self-reliance.

Mission

Make critical capability an Indian strength.

The mission is to create durable industrial capacity in the materials and technologies that shape India's energy transition, digital economy and national security.

That requires patient capital, excellent execution, stronger research institutions, skilled people and infrastructure that lets the next generation of Indian companies build at scale.

Five pillars

01

Economic Strategy & Currency Stability

Promote high-value, high-margin manufacturing in semiconductors, green technology and advanced machinery; deepen domestic capital markets; strengthen the Rupee through local-currency trade and disciplined fiscal policy; and formalize the economy through broader tax participation and access to micro-equity.

02

Human Capital: Education & Health

Shift education toward critical thinking, STEM and technical and vocational learning; scale national R&D intensity toward 3–4% of GDP; and emphasize preventive healthcare, sanitation, clean water, primary care and nutritional security.

03

Environmental Quality & Modern Urbanization

Modernize cities through transit-oriented development, green building standards, intelligent waste systems and resilient utilities; accelerate clean transport, wastewater treatment and regenerative agriculture; and strengthen food standards and cold-chain logistics.

04

Social Cohesion, Inclusivity & Discipline

Bridge inequality through progressive taxation, broad access to digital public infrastructure and asset creation for rural and underprivileged populations, while reinforcing rule of law, civic responsibility and community engagement.

05

Technological & Military Sovereignty

Build self-reliance in strategic technologies and defence, including autonomous systems, AI-enabled electronic warfare, cyber defence and space capabilities, alongside domestic supply chains for critical minerals, advanced computing, biotech and energy storage.

From principle to action

01

Economic reform

Analyse reforms needed to materially lift per-capita GDP and strengthen long-term investment capacity.

02

R&D acceleration

Develop a pathway toward significantly higher national research intensity, including a 5% of GDP long-term objective.

03

21st-century cities

Outline practical urban-planning blueprints built around cleaner mobility, better infrastructure and stronger environmental outcomes.
