About the campus

Integrated by design.

A Platform, not a single plant.

PowerIn Urja India Private Limited (PIU) is developing the PowerIn Integrated Manufacturing Campus (PIMC) at MIDC Additional Butibori, Nagpur, Maharashtra. The wider campus is planned across 300+ acres, with Phase I focused on advanced solar ingot and wafer manufacturing.

The platform is conceived to span a value chain from hyper-pure polysilicon, solar and semiconductor ingots / wafers through devices and critical consumables, neocloud, energy storage, hydrogen-based devices, photonics, mechatronics and selected ancillaries and R&D.
